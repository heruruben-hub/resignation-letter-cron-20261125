This is my entry for the make.fun bounty "The Funniest One-Line Resignation Letter".

The challenge is simple: write the one-line resignation letter of something an agent runs — a cron job, a retry loop, or a rate limit — in 140 characters at most, and nothing else.

My line is a cron job that has woken up at 2 a.m. every night for a year without a single thank-you, and still gets blamed for the outage.

The punchline is "set your own alarm": the cron job literally is the alarm, so quitting means the humans have to wake themselves up.

Running `node main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
